## Decisions (the brief's D1–D24; the lead's call)

| # | Decision |
|---|---|
| D1 | Check-in: one tap on a 9×9 pleasantness × energy grid (valence and energy −4..+4), then an optional stress rating "Not at all, A little, Some, Quite a lot, Very much" (0–4), an optional feeling word and up to 8 context tags. Stored in a new `MoodCheck` table, never in the ledger. `tookMs` is measured so the 5-second claim is checked. |
| D2 | Phone cover widget and tile open a compact sheet (1 + 2 taps). No past value is ever shown on the cover, the tile, a notification or the lock screen. |
| D3 | Today gets a slim "Check in" strip in the side column that opens the same sheet. It never shows a count, a colour judgement or an ask. Body › Mind is the home of the trend. |
| D4 | Prompts come later (phase 6): one a day at most by default, random in 11:00–19:00, opt-in. |
| D5 | No reward of any kind for checking in, or for any mood value (Deci, Koestner & Ryan 1999). |
| D6 | No PHQ-4 or PSS-4 in v1. |
| D7 | Close the day stays as it is while Duty is off. When it renders, its bare 1–5 row is replaced by the grid in a later phase. |
| D8 | Each check-in has its own Delete, plus "Delete all check-ins". The 'life' reset does not touch them, as it doesn't touch weight. |
| D9 | Production has no login, so check-ins are as exposed as the rest of the app (tasks, notes). The lead flags this to the user and recommends a login and the mobile token. Values never go into URLs, push payloads, `/check`, the widget or Gemini. |
| D10, D11 | `ExerciseSession` stays the one header for every exercise kind (ADD COLUMN only; `distanceKm` 0 for kinds with no distance). The WALK-only readers (the brief's H2–H12) are fixed before any non-walk row is written. |
| D12 | LIFT is a record at first: "Counts as" ticks the linked Gym habit, which stays the pay. |
| D13 | Cardio kinds are records until `SESSION_PAY_LAUNCH_DAY` flips (phase 5), after `readLiveSessions` and the momentum sims. |
| D14–D18 | Strength PR Seals carry no XP. e1RM is Brzycki for reps ≤ 10 (unknown RIR counts as 0). Set kinds are WARMUP / WORKING / DROP / MINI, plus `toFailure` and `amrap` flags, with `chainOf`. Bodyweight movements use one signed-load axis. Drop and rest-pause segments count +0.5 hard sets each, at most +1 a chain. |
| D19–D21 | LIFT minutes don't count toward the weekly 150 ("Strength days n of 2" instead). Vitality perks are renamed to sessions when pay flips. Health Connect is widened to cardio and strength sessions (minutes only for strength), with no new permission. |
| D22 | Capture grammars ("mood 2 s3", "bench 3x8 60") come in a later phase, routed before task code. |
| D23 | Body subs become "This week, Exercise, Mind". Exercise becomes multi-kind with a Lift mode. |
| D24 | Phase order, each phase shippable and gated:<br>1. Mind on the web: table, card, Today strip, safety.<br>2. Mind on the phone: Check row, tile, widget, queue.<br>3. Exercise kinds as records, with the WALK-only fixes.<br>4. The lift logger: sets, records, PR Seals, phone SET.<br>5. Health Connect widening, then the pay switch after the sims.<br>6. Capture grammars and prompts. |

## Safety (fixed text)

- Always visible: "Need to talk? Lifeline 13 11 14".
- Low-pattern card: in the last 7 days, at least 5 days had a check-in, and on 5 or more of those days the lowest valence was −3 or below or stress was 4. It shows while the pattern holds until the user hides it; once hidden it stays hidden for 7 days. It is never sent anywhere.
- It lists Lifeline 13 11 14 (text 0477 13 11 14), Beyond Blue 1300 22 4636, the Suicide Call Back Service 1300 659 467 and 000. It suggests a GP.
- The numbers are kept in one constant with a `checkedOn` date (2026-10-27, from AIHW and Lifeline). No diagnosis words, no suicidality item and no alerts to anyone.
