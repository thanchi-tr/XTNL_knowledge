# M2 Duty: the compulsory contract, forgiveness and rituals

**Superseded.** This plan was written before the capture rebuild (capture.md), the redesign (redesign.md), M5 (m5-refit.md, launched 2026-10-01), weight tracking and the keyboard shortcuts. It is no longer the build spec and nothing should be built from it.

Read instead:

- **m2-contracts.md**: the frozen exports every lane builds on (duty-economy.ts, duty-rule.ts, the habit.ts extensions, the type shells).
- **grading.md** E (debt, make-ups, freezes, rest), F (held weeks, pro-rated floors, the DUTY gate) and G (full days paid by the week judge).
- **data-model.md** MIGRATION 2, the dedupe keys, the PendingChange JSON and the second ledger writer.

What changed from this plan, in short (each with its reason in m2-refit.md):
- Day d settles at 04:00 on d + 2 on day keys, never hours, from a launch day the lead sets (DUTY_LAUNCH_DAY), and never in render: after() through one maintenance chain and a daily cron (decisions 1–5).
- A Full day is recorded by settlement and paid its MP by the week judge, after the kept tracks (decision 6).
- A late make-up writes MADE_UP (the debt is cleared, the occurrence stays missed); a minimum make-up pays K 0.3 × T 0.85; a make-up can be undone through a negative DEBT_REPAID 'unrepaid:' row (decisions 8–10).
- Freezes cover no-activity days only; rest, sick and vacation days (30 a year) hold everything except musts marked 'Even on rest days'; standing rest weekdays, the rested bonus, knee reconcile, velocity, stalled goals and WelcomeBack are deferred (decisions 12–14, 33).
- The weekly review runs Saturday to Wednesday on the week reviewedWeek(today) names and never states a provisional verdict (decision 26). The bell lists 'Owed' (counted) and yesterday's open musts (listed, not counted); Today never opens on red (decision 27).
