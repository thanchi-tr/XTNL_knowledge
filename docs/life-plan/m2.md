# M2 Duty: the compulsory contract, forgiveness and rituals

Compulsory tasks acquire real, bounded stakes: XP debt shown as make-up cards, and a streak of their own. The day is judged only after the user has had the whole next day to record it. Freezes, rest, sick and vacation days, minimum versions and one-day repair make every slip recoverable. Close-the-day and a Monday weekly review close the loop. Migration 2 (life_duty: RestDay) ships here.

## Lazy, idempotent settlement and the life cron

**Spec.** src/lib/settlement-plan.ts is pure: planSettlement(state) → ops[]. src/lib/settlement.ts only reads state and executes ops.

settleLifeDays(user, now) judges every day d with settledThroughDay < d ≤ todayKey − 2, i.e. now ≥ dayEndOf(d) + 24 h.
- A null cursor is first set to today − 2 and judges nothing, so M1-era musts never get retroactive debt.
- 'Settle yesterday' in the sheet judges d = yesterday early.

Per day, one $transaction array:
1. Expected instances, using each template's rule as it stood on d. Pending changes apply only after the days before them are settled.
2. Rest, sick or vacation days → EXCUSED, unless compulsoryOnRest.
3. A day with no activity and a banked freeze → FREEZE_USE ('freeze-use:<d>'): held, and all duties EXCUSED.
4. Remaining compulsory misses → MISSED plus DEBT, with the caps.
5. TARGET compulsory tasks at period close → one MISSED slot per unit short.
6. FREEZE_EARN when d is active, there are ≥ 7 active days since the last earn, and the balance is < 2.
7. FULL_DAY when all compulsory tasks are done or excused, the knowledge quest is met (or nothing was due, or ≥ 15 reviews), and there was ≥ 1 life TASK or WORKOUT.
8. REPAIR of d−1 when d−1 broke the streak and d is a FULL_DAY, at most 1 per 7 days.
9. KNEE_RECONCILE ADJUST.
10. Pending changes whose time has come.

Then a conditional updateMany of settledThroughDay. A P2002 means another run already settled that day: re-read and continue.

Where it runs:
- Inline on /today when the cached LifeSettings shows the cursor is behind (once per day, 1-2 round trips), otherwise not at all.
- The new cron route /api/cron/life (vercel.json '15 18 * * *', 04:15 AEST) also runs it, with the same CRON_SECRET check as degrade. It also retries AI sizing (≤ 10) and purges HR buckets from M4. It exports no maxDuration.

There are no interactive transactions.

**Files.** prisma/schema.prisma + prisma/migrations/20261019000000_life_duty/migration.sql; new src/lib/settlement-plan.ts, src/lib/settlement.ts, src/app/api/cron/life/route.ts; edit vercel.json, src/app/today/page.tsx (inline fast-path call)

**Tests.** scripts/settle-check.ts (pure, 40+ scenarios):
- A miss creates exactly 1 DEBT; a second run makes 0 ops; grace not yet passed makes 0 ops.
- A null cursor judges nothing.
- A 4th open miss on one template gives debt 0 'capped'; total open debt over 100 is capped.
- A no-activity day with a freeze → FREEZE_USE and EXCUSED duties; without a freeze → debt.
- Freeze earned on the 7th active day, max 2.
- compulsoryOnRest is not excused.
- TARGET:3/W compulsory with 1 done → 2 MISSED.
- A pending change applies at +7 days, not before.
- FULL_DAY and REPAIR rules; a second repair within 7 days is refused.
- Knee reconcile after an undo.
- A week spanning the Sydney DST change.

## Debt, make-up cards and per-duty streaks

**Spec.** Implements grading section E.

The Must lane lists today's musts first, then make-up cards with a red left border: '−4.2 owed · Dishes · Tue · [Make up] [Do minimum]'. A collapsed 'Owed: 2 · −12.5 XP' row sits at the bottom of the board. There is no red wall at the top, and intact per-duty streaks are highlighted.

makeUp(templateId, day):
- instance DONE_LATE with a TASK event at T 0.85, plus DEBT_REPAID ('repaid:…') for the full debt, in one $transaction array.
- Within 48 h of dayEnd(d) the per-duty streak is restored (repaired = true), at most once per 7 days per template.

doMinimum:
- instance DONE_MVV with K 0.3 and full repayment.
- The per-duty streak is held, not advanced.

Archived templates keep their make-up cards.

acceptLoss is available only when LifeSettings.debtWriteOff is on and the debt is ≥ 14 days old. The instance becomes WRITTEN_OFF, DEBT_WRITTEN_OFF is written, and the DEBT row stays.

After 3 consecutive misses, an inline card offers 'Add a minimum version' or 'Make it easier (takes effect in 7 days)'.

Copy never blames: 'Tuesday's stretch is still open. 10 min makes it right.'

**Files.** new src/app/actions/duty.ts (makeUp, doMinimum, acceptLoss), src/components/today/MakeUpCard.tsx, OwedRow.tsx, MissPrompt.tsx; edit src/components/today/TodayBoard.tsx (lanes), src/lib/habit.ts (DONE_LATE/repaired semantics)

**Tests.** settle-check, duty section:
- Make-up within 48 h → DEBT_REPAID equals the debt, TASK at 0.85, streak restored.
- Make-up at 49 h → streak stays broken, debt cleared.
- MVV repays in full, pays 0.3, and holds the streak.
- acceptLoss is refused when the setting is off or the debt is < 14 days old.
- Ledger invariant: on time +P; missed then made up +0.85·P'; missed −debt.

## Record-yesterday sheet

**Spec.** It opens on the first /today visit of a day while yesterday has open expected instances. It stays available until yesterday is judged, which is the whole of today.
- Header: 'Yesterday's list. Tick what you did.'
- Rows: [Did it] (source record-yesterday, T 1.00) or [Didn't].
- 'Use a freeze for Wed' appears when one is banked; a freeze covers the whole day.

It replaces M1's Yesterday list. Never shown before any charge is made.

## Freezes, rest, sick days, vacation and the rested bonus

**Spec.** - Freezes: balance = EARN − USE events, max 2 banked, shown as pips beside the streak. A freeze is used automatically on a day with no activity, or spent by hand.
- Rest: declared before the day starts. At most 2 per life week, including settings.restWeekdays.
- Sick: can be declared the same day, 1 per 14 days.
- Vacation / pause: up to 30 consecutive days, declared from tomorrow, never retroactive.
- Each of these holds the global streak and the per-duty streaks, and excuses compulsory tasks except those marked compulsoryOnRest.
- The first active day after a REST or VACATION day gets the rested ×1.10 bonus on its first 50 raw XP (grading section C), labelled 'rested'.
- Banners: 'Rest day. Nothing is owed today.' and 'Paused until 12 Oct'.
- computeStreak treats held days as bridging. For an unjudged yesterday with a banked freeze it shows 'a freeze will cover Wed'.

**Files.** new src/components/today/RestControls.tsx; actions in src/app/actions/duty.ts (declareRest, declareSick, setVacation, cancelRest, setRestWeekdays); edit src/lib/activity.ts (held days), src/lib/life-grade.ts (rested), src/components/today/DayLedger.tsx (freezes and streak.broken props)

**Tests.** settle-check:
- Rest declared the same day is rejected. Sick the same day is allowed; a second sick day within 14 days is rejected.
- A 31-day vacation is rejected.
- Rest in the past is rejected.
- Rested bonus: first 50 raw × 1.1, order-invariant.

streak-check: rest, freeze and vacation days bridge the streak.

## Akrasia horizon for weakening commitments

**Spec.** Weakening a compulsory template does not take effect immediately. It is stored as pendingChange with pendingChangeAt = now + 7 days and shows 'Takes effect Thu 8 Oct'. Weakening means:
- turning compulsory off;
- fewer days, or a longer EVERY interval;
- a lower TARGET;
- a later deadline;
- archiving.

Exceptions:
- Strengthening applies immediately.
- Edits within 60 min of creation apply immediately (typo grace).
- Rest, sick and vacation remain immediate escapes.

Settlement applies the change only after settling the days before it.

**Files.** src/app/actions/tasks.ts (editTemplate classifies strengthen vs weaken), src/lib/settlement-plan.ts (apply), src/components/today/TaskDrawer.tsx (pending badge)

**Tests.** settle-check:
- Un-flagging at day 0 still judges days 1-6 as compulsory and stops at day 7.
- A typo edit at 59 min applies immediately; at 61 min it is deferred.
- Adding a day applies immediately.

## Close the day and the Monday weekly review

**Spec.** CloseDaySheet is optional. It is prominent after 18:00 and a small link otherwise.
- Open non-compulsory todos: [Tomorrow] [Anytime] [Drop], plus 'Roll all to tomorrow'. Planned items carry forward and are never late.
- Open musts show 'Do the minimum (10 pushups · 5 min)'.
- An optional one-line note and a mood of 1-5 are stored as a REFLECTION event (sink NONE, never graded, not counted for the streak).
- A 'Rest tomorrow' toggle.

/today/week (Saturday to Monday, with a Monday badge as the fresh-start landmark). Every step can be skipped:
1. Last week: life XP by track, days shown up, compulsory kept % with each duty's streak and habit rung, freezes used, and velocity per category ('admin tasks take 1.4× your estimate', or 'calibrating n/10').
2. Inbox to zero.
3. Goals check-in: stalled 14 days, MANUAL progress, reschedule or close.
4. Owed: make up, or accept the loss if enabled.
5. Next week: rest days and capacity minutes.

Notices (buildFeed): 'Owed: 2 · −12.5 XP' (bad) and 'Record yesterday: 3' (warn). NavTodayLink gets a red dot when debt is open.

**Files.** new src/components/today/CloseDaySheet.tsx, src/app/today/week/page.tsx, src/app/actions/rituals.ts; edit src/lib/notifications.ts, src/components/NavTodayLink.tsx

**Tests.** board-check, rituals section:

- The velocity median only appears after ≥ 10 timed completions.
- Notice derivation: debt gives 'bad' and is actionable.

settle-check: a reflection never changes XP or the streak.
