# M5 Character: life tracks, goals and mastery

Superseded for the build by `m5-refit.md`. That is the spec every lane builds from, and `m5-contracts.md` freezes the signatures. This page says what M5 ships. The constants live in `src/lib/life-economy.ts` and the formulas in `grading.md` sections F and G.

Life moves the one character:
- Body, Duty, Craft and Care levels are earned from TRACK XP and capped by kept weeks and paid goals.
- They feed the 13 attributes, the emblem gates, the title epithet and a single character level.
- Goals state their mastery points (MP) when they are created and pay them, within caps, when they are closed.
- The You sheet, You › Stats, /today/week and /today/rules show it.

There is no migration. M2, M3 and M4 are absent, so there is no settlement cron, no debt, no rest days, no workouts and no PRs. Everything is inert until the lead sets `LIFE_LAUNCH_DAY`.

## What changed from the first M5 plan

| First plan | Ships |
|---|---|
| A Sunday step in M2's settlement writes WEEK rows | A lazy, idempotent week judge runs in `after()` on page reads. M2's settlement will call the same function |
| Judged with Sunday's settlement, at Tuesday 04:00 | Week W is judged from Wednesday 04:00 after its Sunday. That is one day later than the record window needs, so M2's 48-hour make-ups are never too late |
| depth = √keptWeeks + goal depth (MID 1, LONG 3) + min(3, 0.1 × PRs) | depth = 1.25·√keptWeeks + min(2, goal depth) (MID 1, LONG 2). There is no PR term |
| BODY floor: WHO minutes and 2 strength days from workouts | BODY floor: 150 effort minutes from EXERCISE-category receipts |
| LIFE_PR 0.5 MP; LIFE_FULL_DAY minted in M5 | LIFE_PR is reserved and never minted. LIFE_FULL_DAY is minted from M2, through the same cap |
| REVIEWS, IDEAS, WORKOUTS and RUN_KM goals measured | Only CHILDREN and MANUAL goals are measured. The rest read 'not measured' and pay 0 |
| `scripts/backfill-weeks.ts` | Not written. The judge itself writes pre-launch weeks as 'backfill · …' rows with no MP and no Seal |
| /overview CharacterPanel with recharts | The You sheet's life section, Stats cards, /today/week and /today/rules, with no recharts |
| Goals open at launch get goalMp then | Unchanged. New goals freeze goalMp at creation (phase B) |

## Weekly judgement

- **When.** Week W, Monday to Sunday in life days, can be judged from Wednesday 04:00 after its Sunday: `lastJudgeableSunday(today)` is the Sunday on or before today − 3. The epoch week is judged on its days ≥ epochDay.
- **How.** `maybeJudgeWeeks(userId)` runs in `after()` on /you and /today/week (phase A) and on /today (phase B).
  - It returns at once unless writes are enabled, life is launched, epochDay is set, and the latest judgeable week is missing from the cached ledger.
  - It runs once per user at a time and never throws.
  - `judgeClosedWeeks` plans at most 12 weeks per run, oldest first. It commits one `$transaction` array per week: four WEEK rows plus that week's mints.
  - A P2002 means another run judged the week first: it stops, and the next run re-reads.
  - Rendering twice, or on two devices, never pays twice.
- **Rows.** One WEEK row per track:
  - dedupeKey `'week:<TRACK>:<YYYY-Www>'`, qty 1 kept or 0, sink NONE, track set, day = the week's Sunday;
  - detail = the reason line, for example 'Kept · 4 days · 52.0 raw XP · 180 effort min' or 'Not kept · 2 of 3 days · 12.0 of 30 raw XP'.
- **Backfill.** A week whose Sunday is before the launch day is written 'backfill · …'. It counts for depth, but it mints no MP and plays no Seal.

## Kept-week floors

The inputs are live TASK rows with sink TRACK. An UNDO removes the row it undoes. #play, study-linked and other sink-NONE completions never count.
- **CRAFT and CARE:** completions on ≥ 3 distinct days and ≥ 30 raw XP.
- **BODY:** the same, plus ≥ 150 effort minutes.
  - Effort minutes come from EXERCISE-category receipts only, as minutes × the weight of the receipt's band: INTRO 0, STANDARD 1, DEMANDING and SEVERE 2.
  - HEALTH minutes never count.
- **DUTY:** any missed compulsory occurrence breaks the week. Otherwise:
  - with ≥ 3 occurrences, Duty raw must be ≥ 30;
  - with 0–2 occurrences, it needs ≥ 5 Duty completions on ≥ 3 days and raw ≥ 30.
  - An MVV holds its must and still needs the floor.
- Held days, from rest or vacation, are an empty input until M2. No week is 'held' in M5.

## Levels, attributes and the character

- pointsLevel = floor(√xp / 7).
- depth = 1.25·√keptWeeks + min(2, goal depth), where a paid MID adds 1 and a paid LONG adds 2.
- level = min(pointsLevel, 1 + floor(depth)). Every track starts at 0.
- Life feeds the attributes as one row per track with level > 0, named 'Life · Body' and so on.
  - Its level is L × (1 + streakBonusPercent(7 × kept streak)/100). The bonus reaches +20% after 10 kept weeks and is never amplified by STREAK_AMPLIFIER.
  - Its composition is the track's seed blended with its tasks' compositions by XP.
  - Emblem gates and 'the Unwearied' follow the scores automatically.
- characterLevel = floor(Σ over Fields and tracks of L^0.75), using the plain track level.
- `life-tracks-server.ts` (`loadLifeLedger`, `loadLifeTracks`) is the only reader of track levels.

## Life MP and goals

- LIFE_WEEK_KEPT pays 1.5 per kept track, minted on the week's Sunday.
- The cap is 8 MP per life week, shared by LIFE_WEEK_KEPT, GOAL_SHORT and (from M2) LIFE_FULL_DAY.
  - The trim order is: Short goals at close, then kept tracks (Body, Duty, Craft, Care), then full days.
  - M5 can pay at most 4 × 1.5 + 2 × 1 = 8 in a week, so it never trims.
- Goals:

| Horizon | Stated | Pays | Min lifetime | Limit | Depth |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SHORT | 1 | 1, only at g = 1 | 3 d | ≤ 2 paying per life week | 0 |
| MID | 6 | 6 × g from g ≥ 0.7 | 21 d | ≤ 2 paying per rolling 30 d | 1 |
| LONG | 20 | 20 × g from g ≥ 0.7 | 90 d | ≤ 1 paying per rolling 91 d | 2 |

- Closing a goal is explicit and final.
  - g is measured as of min(close day, due day).
  - Every close writes exactly one MP_MINT decision row `'mp:GOAL:<id>'`, with qty equal to the MP paid (0 allowed) and detail = reason · why. A MasteryLedgerEntry is written only when it pays.
- A missed goal reads 'Carried 0.55', with no debt.
- Goals never pay XP and never write TRACK rows.
- No MP is paid for a week whose Sunday is before the launch day, nor for any goal before launch.

## What the user sees

- **You sheet.**
  - The track rows: sigil, level, 8-week pips, a meter with banked and now, a cap tick when capped, and an honest line such as '1,960 / 2,401 XP · depth cap 7 · 7 more kept weeks raise it'.
  - 'life MP this week {used}/8' in the hero.
  - The 'Goals and mastery' card with the goal ladder.
  - A one-time note: 'Life now counts toward your character'.
- **Stats:** 'Track levels' over up to 12 judged weeks, and 'Kept weeks'.
- **/today/week:** the last judged week, with each track's reason, the MP paid and the streak.
- **/today/rules:** a 'Tracks and kept weeks' card rendered from life-economy.
- **Celebrations:**
  - the week Seal states its exact MP;
  - a track level-up folds into it, or into a character-level or title moment;
  - a goal-finished Seal plays only at or above its bar and states what was paid, never goalMp;
  - a one-time launch moment.

## Launch and gates

- `LIFE_LAUNCH_DAY` (a code constant, null until the lead sets it) is the first life day M5 counts.
  - A code constant survives a 'life' reset, which a LifeSettings stamp would not.
  - Outside production, `XTNL_LIFE_LAUNCH_DAY` overrides it, for the rehearsal server.
- Writes on read need `NODE_ENV=production` or `XTNL_LIFE_JUDGE=1`. Dev and prod share one database, so a local dev server never writes WEEK or MP rows.
- `scripts/life-launch.ts` (lead only):
  - The dry run prints the week plan with its backfill flags, goalMp for open goals, the character and title before and after, the life attribute shares, the gates life newly opens, and the decay check.
  - `--apply` runs `judgeClosedWeeks(force)` and `stateGoalMp`, writes one zero-delta DECAY_GRACE row, and plays the launch moment.
  - Every write is idempotent.

## Economy guard

`npm run balance:horizon` prints a LIFE block and exits 1 on any failed assertion. The assertions are:
1. Worst case 8/7 + 6 × 2/30 + 20 × 1/91 = 1.7626 MP/day ≤ 3% of the 61.964 MP/day knowledge income (1.8589).
2. The pool horizon stays ≥ 10 y. It is 13.33 y in the worst case, against 13.71 y for knowledge alone; the committed 1.209 MP/day gives 13.45 y.
3. M5's weekly maximum, 4 × 1.5 + 2 × 1, equals the cap of 8.
4. The steady basket is 7.5 tasks/day and 101.5 XP per track-week.
5. Steady pacing: level 4 at week 9, level 5 at week 13, and level 10 at week 49.
6. Light pacing: level 10 at week 81.
7. Heavy volume buys at most one level at week 52.
8. The worst case is level 6 at week 13, and reaches level 10 only at week 32.
9. Life alone keeps every attribute below the tier-5 gate: SELF_RESPECT 13.82 < 14.2.

A bumped weekly cap (30), goal-depth cap (3) or third Mid per 30 days each exits 1.
