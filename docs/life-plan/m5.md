# M5 Character: life tracks, goals and mastery

Life moves the one character:
- Body, Duty, Craft and Care levels are capped by kept weeks, goals and PRs.
- They feed the 13 attributes, emblem gates, title epithet and a single character level.
- Goals pay stated, capped mastery points.
- /overview shows mastery across knowledge and life over months.
No migration.

## Weekly judgement: kept weeks with effort floors

**Spec.** The settlement planner gains a Sunday step. For each track it writes WEEK ('week:<track>:<weekKey>', qty 1 kept or 0, sink NONE), using the kept-week rules and effort floors in grading section F. Vacation weeks are skipped.

A one-time scripts/backfill-weeks.ts (dry-run first) writes WEEK events for closed weeks since epochDay. They count for depth only, carry detail 'backfill', and never mint MP.

## Life mastery points and goal payouts

**Spec.** mastery.ts mintLifeMasteryOp(userId, reason, delta, detail) returns the paired op array (an MP_MINT event with its dedupe key, plus the MasteryLedgerEntry). Settlement mints:
- LIFE_WEEK_KEPT 1.5 per kept track;
- LIFE_FULL_DAY 0.5;
- LIFE_PR 0.5, only for PRs from sensor sessions;
- GOAL_SHORT 1.0.
These are trimmed by the 8-per-life-week cap.

src/lib/goals.ts (pure):
- horizonFor; progress for CHILDREN, MANUAL, REVIEWS/IDEAS (ledger counts, credit only), WORKOUTS and RUN_KM (Workout rows).
- g = min(1, progress/target) at the due day or when marked done.
- payout(horizon, g, lifetime, counts):
  - SHORT: 1 MP, binary, lifetime ≥ 3 d, ≤ 2 per week.
  - MID: 6 × g, when g ≥ 0.7, lifetime ≥ 21 d, ≤ 2 per 30 d; adds depth 1.
  - LONG: 20 × g, when g ≥ 0.7, lifetime ≥ 90 d, ≤ 1 per 91 d; adds depth 3.
- goalMp is frozen and displayed when the goal is created. Goals open at M5 launch get it then.
- A missed aspirational goal shows 'Carried 0.55. Reschedule or close?' with no debt.
- Goals never pay XP.
- No MP for weeks or goals closed before launch.

**Files.** src/lib/mastery.ts, new src/lib/goals.ts, src/lib/settlement-plan.ts (mint ops), src/app/actions/tasks.ts (closeGoal)

**Tests.** character-check:

- MID at g 0.69 pays 0; at g 0.8 it pays 4.8 plus depth 1; a 3rd MID within 30 days pays 0.
- LONG with a lifetime under 90 days pays 0.
- A REVIEWS goal creates no TRACK xp.

## Economy guard: balance-horizon life block

**Spec.** scripts/balance-horizon.ts imports the life MP constants and prints a LIFE block:
- the committed life income, about 1.2 MP/day (weekly cap binding, plus 1 MID per quarter);
- the capped worst case, 1.76 MP/day;
- the whole-pool horizon with and without life income: 13.7 y → about 13.3 y.

It exits 1 if the worst case exceeds 3% of the committed knowledge income (61.96 MP/day) or the horizon drops below 10 y.

**Files.** scripts/balance-horizon.ts

**Tests.** npm run balance:horizon prints the LIFE block and exits 0. A temporary constant bump (weekly cap 30) makes it exit 1; verify locally, then revert.
