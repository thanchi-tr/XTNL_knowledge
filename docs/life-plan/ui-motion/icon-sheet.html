Icon sheet — /dev/style/glyphs

The closed glyph set that replaces explanatory sentences: 24 × 24, currentColor, round caps, ≤ 6 paths, and states that differ in shape, never colour alone (a started pip, a solid fill, a check badge, an extra stone). Use the Motion toggle in the bar above to see each motion in Full, Calm · reduced (opacity only, ≤ 260 ms, no flourish, no loop, no shader) and Still (nothing moves; the end state and the same words). The animate() counter in the bar stays at 0 under Still. Reserved line meanings: dashed = calibrating or not yet counted · dotted = from your ticks · ≈ = estimate · the balloon = not checked · struck = avoided, dropped, closed or pays nothing.

Shaders · horizon (AMBIENT) and weave (WAIT) with their SVG first paint

Each band is two SVG layers in the first paint: the soft layer (dawn or strands) and, for the horizon, the measured marks on top. A canvas replaces only the soft layer, only while the slot loops, after load + 400 ms of quiet + 50% in view. DPR is capped at 1 (horizon) and 1.5 (weave); offscreen holds, 10 s offscreen releases, a hidden tab holds, and Calm, Still, high contrast, no WebGL and power save stay on the SVG. Toggle them in the bar and watch the status lines.

The horizon's fragment program has no uniform that carries Proficiency and no path code: it cannot move a measured mark. At air 0 the canvas equals the SVG dawn within 1/255, so the swap in and out is invisible.
