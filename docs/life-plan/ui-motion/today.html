Today — the aim line and week quests

Today is the most visited screen, so it stays the calmest (D10): no shader, no loop, no burst. What carries the motion is the quest glyph itself: a quest kind is a shape (book + chevron = Bring, card + plus = Add, the plan's own track sigil = Practice, a stair = Step, a target = Checkpoint) with its evidence badge bottom-right; it gains a solid started pip at 1+ and a check badge when done. The only things that move here are a meter from its last-seen value and the check drawing in when a quest completes (SEEN, once). The brief's “tick burst” stays where rarity earns it: the Aim-rank rise is the roadmap's only burst (H10), and it plays on /you, never on /today.
