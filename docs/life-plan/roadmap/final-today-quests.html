Today — the week quests card

Each started milestone is sliced into this life week (Monday 04:00 to Monday 04:00, Sydney): concrete actions whose counts code works out from the milestone's own gap, the weeks left, what the review schedule lets cards reach, and the time the app has seen you keep. The app's own rows verify each one: cards your reviews brought to the level (tested), cards you added (counted by the app), and sessions and steps you ticked. A week quest has no checkbox, pays nothing, and is frozen for its week. On Today it is a quiet card under the goals, headed Week quests · Milestone 2 (the request's working name "This week's quests" would put a bare "quests" on screen, which the naming rules ban).
