Set an aim — /you/roadmap/new

The intake page. The user writes an Aim in their own words and picks its Area (a Field it grows, or a life track for a practice-only aim), then gives what the planner genuinely needs: a date, hours a week, a starting point and an intensity. Optional fields carry the facts only the user can supply (constraints, an exam, the official syllabus with its source, and "hours this usually takes" with its source). Every figure under a field is the app's own reading of the user's records, never a model's. Revision 3 changes two things here: an aim's date is at most 3 years away (the "Beyond this plan" tail is cut), and the privacy line gains the free-tier note, because this server's Gemini key is on Google's free tier.
