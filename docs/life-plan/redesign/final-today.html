How a day is judged

  1. A day is judged after you have had the whole next day to record it.
  2. Rest, sick and vacation days are held: nothing is owed.
  3. A day with no activity uses a banked freeze if you have one.
  4. A missed must is owed once, capped. It never grows.
  5. A Full day (musts, quest, one life deed) pays 0.5 MP and can repair the broken day before it, once a week.
