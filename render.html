Procedural Pixel Art Architecture Testbench

1. Claude Default (Procedural Bevel)

Flaws:
• High-frequency spatial noise (every brick independently beveled)
• Linear RGB darkening (muddy desaturated shadows, 0° hue shift)
• Zero roof occlusion (wall lit immediately under overhang)
• Radial/pillow shading on roof cone

2. Production Standard (Volume + Clustered)

Fixes:
• Directional global lighting vector (dx=-1, dy=-1, dz=1)
• 5-step CIELAB hue-shifted ramps (sky-tinted highlights, navy shadows)
• 4px hard overhang cast shadow with 1px half-tone feather
• Clustered masonry breaks (< 10% wall area) preserving macro form
